# Question 1

## 题干

The surface area of a solid sphere of radius $R$ is equal to the total surface area of 10 solid closed cylinders of radius $r$ and height $4r$.

Which of the following is an expression for $R$ in terms of $r$?

(The surface area of a sphere of radius $R$ is $4\pi R^2$.)

## 选项

**A** $R = 5r$

**B** $R = 12r$

**C** $R = 2\sqrt{5}\,r$

**D** $R = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{10}\,r$

**E** $R = \sqrt{10}\,r$

**F** $R = \dfrac{3}{2}\sqrt{10}\,r$

**G** $R = \sqrt{15}\,r$
