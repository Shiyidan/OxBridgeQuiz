# Question 2

## 题干

A spaceship of mass $10\,000\,\text{kg}$ is moving at $2.0\,\text{m s}^{-1}$ relative to a space station.

The spaceship is captured by a robotic arm attached to the space station and brought to rest by a force of $1000\,\text{N}$.

How far will the spaceship move in its initial direction relative to the space station while the force is being applied?

(Assume that the acceleration of the space station is negligible.)

## 选项

**A** $0.050\,\text{m}$

**B** $0.10\,\text{m}$

**C** $0.20\,\text{m}$

**D** $5.0\,\text{m}$

**E** $10\,\text{m}$

**F** $20\,\text{m}$
